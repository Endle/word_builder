A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Every letter counts.

Enter 2–20 English letters, including duplicates. Choose a word length, then add any starting letters, ending letters, or a full pattern. An underscore or question mark matches one unknown letter.

For example, AERSTIN with a five-letter pattern _ R _ _ _ can produce TRAIN.

Results come from the bundled ENABLE word list (2–15 letters). Frequency rankings use Hermit Dave’s English FrequencyWords data. “Common only” keeps entries among its 15,000 most frequent terms; this is a frequency estimate, not a separate dictionary.

“Show alternatives” remembers suggestions for the same letter set during this visit, even when you change filters. Reset or “Show previous suggestions again” clears that history.
