ENABLE English word list
Source: https://github.com/dolph/dictionary/blob/master/enable1.txt
ENABLE is a public-domain word list. Bundled entries are limited to 2–15 letters.

Frequency ranking
Source: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/content/2018/en/en_50k.txt
English 2018 frequency list, based on OpenSubtitles data. Ranks are used only for words also present in ENABLE. Common means rank <= 15000.
